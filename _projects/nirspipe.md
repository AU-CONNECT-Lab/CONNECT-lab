NIRSPipe is an open-source BIDS App for fNIRS data, led by Qiuyu Yu, a graduate student in the CONNECT Lab. It integrates preprocessing, postprocessing, hyperscanning analysis, and quality control into a single reproducible pipeline that any lab can run with one command. We welcome inquiries from potential collaborators and are happy to offer educational workshops to anyone interested.
