About the Lab

Where Brain, Behavior, and Environment Meet

Our research examines how adolescents' everyday relationships and broader social environments shape brain and body development. By combining neuroimaging (fMRI, fNIRS), physiological measures (ECG, EDA), behavioral observations, and computational approaches, we seek to understand how young people interact with their social worlds. We also develop research methods to better capture these complex relationships.

Our Philosophy

CONNECT Is More Than Our Name.

People connect.

Adolescents' relationships with family, friends, and others play an important role in shaping their development.

Contexts connect.

Families, peer networks, neighborhoods, and broader social environments intersect and jointly shape young people's experiences.

Methods connect.

Bringing together different research approaches allows us to ask questions that no single method can answer alone.

We connect.

We welcome students and fellow researchers who share our curiosity about how social experiences shape the developing brain and body.
