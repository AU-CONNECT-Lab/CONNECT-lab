About the Lab

Where brain, behavior,
and environment meet

CONNECT Lab studies how the environments adolescents grow up in — their neighborhoods, families, and everyday relationships — shape the developing brain and body. We use neurophysiological methods, including functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS), to capture how social experiences translate into biological change during the critical window of adolescence.

Our work sits at the intersection of developmental science, neuroscience, and family studies — asking not just what teens experience, but how those experiences get inside them.
