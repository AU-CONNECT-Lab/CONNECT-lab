bio: |
  My name is Janishi Hightower, and I am entering the HDFS graduate program in the Fall of 2026. I am a former educator with additional certification in early intervention for children from birth through age five. I also previously ran a business designing and creating developmentally informed learning materials for infants and toddlers. Through these experiences, I became increasingly interested in understanding developmental trajectories and the ways life experiences shape them across the life course. I am particularly interested in how adverse experiences, such as poverty and chronic stress, can impact neural and physiological development and, in turn, shape life outcomes. Using fNIRS, I hope to explore the parent-child relationship and the potential buffering effects positive parent-child interactions may have for children experiencing chronic stress. Outside of my academic interests, I enjoy spending time with my husband and three children, hiking, visiting the panhandle, and exploring restaurants and shows in Atlanta.
