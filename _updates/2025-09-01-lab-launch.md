We are thrilled to officially launch CONNECT Lab in the Department of Human Development 
and Family Science at Auburn University! 

Dr. Cai joins Auburn as an Assistant Professor, bringing expertise in adolescent 
neurophysiology and social-contextual influences on development. We are excited to 
begin building our research community here in Auburn.

**We are actively recruiting** PhD students and undergraduate research assistants for 
the 2025–2026 academic year. See our [Join Us](/contact) page for more details.
